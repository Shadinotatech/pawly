## Full description (max 4000)
Never miss a vaccine, pill or flea treatment again.

Pawly is a simple pet care tracker for dogs, cats and every pet in your family. Keep all health records in one place and get reminders right on time.

KEY FEATURES
• Medicine reminders – set dose and times; mark each dose as given with one tap
• Vaccine tracker – record every vaccine and get reminded 3 days before and on the due date
• Repeat care – flea & tick, deworming, grooming, bath, nail trim or any custom task
• Weight tracker with a chart to spot changes early
• Vet visit notes – reason, advice and cost for every visit
• Today screen – see everything due today and this week
• Multiple pets – add as many pets as you have, with photos
• Works offline – no account, no login, no ads
• Private – your data stays on your phone
• Spending tracker – log food, vet, grooming and more; see the monthly total
• Light and dark theme, kg or lb, your own currency

PAWLY PRO (optional)
• Unlimited pets (free plan: 3 pets)
• Vet-ready PDF health report to share with your vet, sitter or boarding
• Spending insights: 6-month chart and spending by category
• Medicine dose history and on-time score

WHY PAWLY
Most pet apps need your vet to be on their system. Pawly works on its own: you stay in control of your pet's records, and reminders arrive even without internet.

Perfect for new puppy and kitten owners, multi-pet households and pets on long-term medicine.

Pawly is a record keeping and reminder tool and does not give veterinary advice. Always follow your vet's advice.

## Answers for Play Console forms

### Data safety
- Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types? **No**
  (All data stays on the device. Nothing is sent to a server.)
- Do you provide a way for users to request that their data is deleted? Users delete data inside the app (Settings > Delete all data) or by uninstalling.

### Content rating questionnaire
- Category: choose **"All Other App Types"** (utility / productivity).
- Violence, sexual content, profanity, drugs, gambling: **No** to all.
- Users can interact or share content with each other: **No**
- Shares user location: **No**
- Digital purchases: **Yes** (Pawly Pro via Google Play Billing)
Expected rating: Everyone / PEGI 3.

### Target audience and content
- Target age group: **18 and over** (and optionally 13–17). Do NOT select under 13.
- App could appeal to children? **No**

### Ads
- Does your app contain ads? **No**

### App access
- All functionality is available without special access: **Yes** (no login).

### Health apps declaration (if asked)
- The app is not a medical device and does not provide human health features. It is a pet record and reminder tool.

### Exact alarm permission (if asked)
- The app uses SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM so medicine reminders arrive on time. The user can allow or deny it in Settings; the app still works with inexact reminders.

### Government app / financial features / news: **No** to all.
