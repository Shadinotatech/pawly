Pawly Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Pawly ("the app") is a pet care tracker that helps you record vaccines, medicines, care tasks, weight and vet visits for your pets, and reminds you when something is due. This policy explains what information the app handles.

Information the app stores

The app stores the information you enter, such as pet names, photos you choose, vaccine and medicine records, weights, vet visit notes and reminder times. This information is saved only on your device.

Information we collect

We do not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. The app has no account, no login, no advertising and no analytics. Your pet records are never sent to our servers.

Photos

If you add a photo of your pet, the app opens the Android photo picker. Only the photo you select is copied into the app's private storage on your device. The app does not access your other photos.

Notifications

The app uses local notifications created on your device to remind you about medicines, vaccines and care tasks. No notification data leaves your device.

Backups

Android may include app data in your device backup to your own Google account if you have device backup turned on. This is controlled by your Android settings and Google's privacy policy. You can also copy a text backup from the app's Settings screen; where you keep that copy is up to you.

Deleting your data

You can delete all data from Settings > Delete all data, or by uninstalling the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Medical disclaimer

Pawly is a record keeping and reminder tool. It does not provide veterinary advice. Always follow the advice of your veterinarian.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
